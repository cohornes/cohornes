# 💫 About Me:
I'm a Ghanaian engineer pursuing my MSc in Computer Science at KIIT University (Bhubaneswar, India), following a BSc in Computer Science from the University of Energy and Natural Resources, Ghana. Over roughly five years I've worked across ICT system administration, QA test analysis, technical support, and AI engineering, which means I've seen systems from the operator's chair, the tester's chair, and the builder's chair. That shapes how I design: I care about what breaks in production, not just what works in a demo.<br><br>I'm also the founder and CEO of JK Cohornes Events, which keeps me honest about shipping, budgets, and real users.<br><br>🔭 What I'm Working On<br>MSc thesis: a five-layer, blockchain-backed AI intrusion detection system aimed at MSSPs:<br>LSTM/GNN anomaly detection<br>Adversarial hardening with IBM ART<br>A Hyperledger Fabric forensic ledger<br>Smart-contract-driven response<br>A federated-learning self-healing loop<br>Python automation portfolio: practical tools built around ML pipelines, ATS-style CV scoring, and LLM-assisted workflows.<br>Algorithmic trading: an automated MetaTrader 5 bot built in Python.
